Things to do with your partner online

Long distance or just a busy week — here are twelve ideas for online dates that are more fun than another “how was your day”. Most of them you can do right in Duet, with your video call on the same screen.

Start free — no sign-up →

Movie and video ideas

  1. Movie night in sync — same film, same second, emoji flying over the screen. How it works.
  2. Bad movie night — pick the worst-rated film you can find and roast it with masks and speech bubbles.
  3. YouTube rabbit hole — take turns choosing the next video.
  4. Guess the plot — pause and both predict what happens next.

Game ideas

  1. Air hockey tournament — best of five, loser plans the next date.
  2. Team challenge — keep the balloon up for 30 hits together.
  3. “Great minds” quiz — see how many answers match.
  4. Draw each other on a shared canvas and save the masterpiece.

Cosy ideas

  1. Cook the same recipe with the camera on.
  2. Silent co-working — study or work together, chat in breaks.
  3. Plan your next visit — share a screen with maps and tickets.
  4. Build a memory album — Duet saves photos and clips of your funniest moments.

Questions

Which of these are free in Duet?

All the movie and game ideas above work in Duet for free, with no sign-up.

Do we need special equipment?

Just two devices with a camera and internet. A computer helps for screen sharing.
