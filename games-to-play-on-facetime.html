The problem with playing over FaceTime

On a normal call you can only talk, and if you open a game in another app the call shrinks to a tiny picture or drops. Many online games also need accounts, downloads and an invite system.

Duet is built the other way round: the video call is the base, and the games open on top of it. You keep seeing each other while the puck flies.

Ideas for your next call

  • Quick duels (2–3 minutes): air hockey, pong, penalty shoot-out, darts.
  • Laugh-out-loud: tug of war, reaction battle, face games that use your camera.
  • Calm and cosy: tic-tac-toe, drawing on a shared canvas, “Great minds” questions.
  • Team play: keep the balloon in the air together, breakout as a team.

Works across devices

FaceTime only works between Apple devices. Duet works in any modern browser — iPhone with Android, a laptop with a phone. Open duetplay.app, send the link, and you are both in.

Questions

Is Duet a FaceTime app?

No. Duet is an independent web app with its own video call, so it works on iPhone, Android and computers alike. FaceTime is a trademark of Apple Inc.

Can we play while still seeing each other?

Yes — your faces stay on screen during the games.
