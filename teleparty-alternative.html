How Duet is different

  • No browser extension — Duet is a web app that runs in any modern browser, on phones too.
  • Video call included — you see each other while you watch.
  • Any source — share a tab or screen, play your own file, or use YouTube and direct links.
  • Reactions on the film — emoji, masks on actors, throwing things, speech bubbles and sounds.
  • Games — 46 two-player games when you want a break from the film.

When an extension is better

If you both have the same streaming subscription and only want perfectly synced playback in a big browser window, a sync extension does that job well. Duet shines when you want to see each other and have fun around the film.

Try it

Open duetplay.app, type a name and send the link. It takes less than a minute and nobody needs an account.

Questions

Is Duet affiliated with Teleparty or Rave?

No. Teleparty and Rave are trademarks of their respective owners; Duet is an independent app.

Can I watch Netflix in Duet?

You can share a browser tab from a computer, but many streaming services block that and your partner will see a black screen. Files, YouTube and links work fine.
