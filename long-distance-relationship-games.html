Why games help when you are far apart

Talking every evening is lovely, but after a few months of long distance many couples run out of “so, how was your day”. Playing something together gives you a shared moment instead of a report: you laugh at the same goal, argue about the same move and remember it the next day.

In Duet the video call is always on while you play, so you still see each other’s faces — the game is something you do together, not a reason to stare at different screens.

What you can play

  • Fast arcade duels — air hockey, pong, tanks, snakes, football, darts, basketball, penalty shoot-out.
  • Team-up games — keep a balloon in the air together, break bricks as a team.
  • Party classics — rock-paper-scissors, tug of war, tic-tac-toe, reaction battles.
  • Getting-to-know-you games — “Great minds”: answer the same question and see if you match.
  • A shared canvas — draw together on one board.

No partner online right now? Every arcade game also works against a bot, or both of you on one phone.

Movie nights too

Besides games, Duet can play a film for both of you in sync: share a browser tab or your screen from a computer, open a video file, or paste a YouTube link. Pause and seek are shared, and you can throw emoji, masks and silly effects at the screen while you watch. See watch movies together online.

How to start in 30 seconds

  1. Open duetplay.app and type your name.
  2. Send the invite link to your partner (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram — anything).
  3. When they open it, the video call starts and you pick a game.

It works in the browser on Android, iPhone and computers. You can also add it to your home screen like an app.

Questions

Is Duet free?

Yes, everything described on this page is free and needs no account — just a name.

Do we both need to install something?

No. Duet runs in the browser. Installing the shortcut on your home screen is optional.

Can we play from different countries?

Yes. Duet connects you directly, so it works across countries and time zones as long as both of you have internet.

What if my partner is on iPhone and I am on Android?

That is fine — Duet works in Safari and Chrome, on phones and computers, in any combination.
