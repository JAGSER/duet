Online games for couples

Whether you live together and want something new for the evening, or you are miles apart, Duet has quick two-player games you can start in seconds — with your faces on screen so every win and every fail is shared.

Compete or play as a team

Some nights you want to beat your partner at air hockey; others you would rather work together. Duet has both: head-to-head duels and co-op games where you win or lose as a team.

Games that make you talk

“Great minds” asks you both the same question — favourite holiday, first thing you would save in a fire, the best pizza topping — and shows if your answers match. It is a fun way to learn small things about each other, even after years together.

On two phones or on one

Play on two devices over the internet, or put one phone on the table and play face to face — the screen splits so each of you has your own side.

Keep the memories

Duet quietly snaps photos and short clips of your funniest reactions, with your names and the game on them. You can save them or share them later.

Questions

Do we need accounts?

Not at all — friends and family play it too. It is simply made for two people.
